About the Photographer | Annie Shi

Hi! I'm Annie Shi, a competitive rower and senior at Brookline High School and the photographer behind Annie Shi Productions. Outside of photography and being on the water, I'm passionate about mathematical economics and quantitative problem-solving. I also run a financial literacy nonprofit, Market Minds (marketmindsyouth.org)! I love talking to new people and hearing stories from fellow BHS alumni, so whether you'd like to share a favorite high school memory or connect in the mathematical economics or financial literacy space, I'd love to hear from you!
